# Demo video scripts

Three short recordings: one per user role, plus a 60-second overview.

**Before recording:**
- Use only the **sample applicant (Jordan Ellis)**.
- Close other tabs and turn on Do Not Disturb.
- Never show the Render Environment page or any API key.
- Record with Loom, Zoom or the built-in recorder (macOS: Shift-Cmd-5; Windows: Win-Alt-R), and turn on auto-captions.
- The on-screen text below doubles as captions for viewers watching with the sound off.

**Before you hand them over:** test each video on a classmate outside the team. It is done when they can complete the task after watching it once.

---

## Video 1: Applicant walkthrough (2–3 min)

| Time | Show | Say / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | The live link on a phone or laptop | "This is for anyone applying to Invest Detroit's Real Estate/C&I program. It replaces guessing at questions and waiting for follow-up emails." |
| 0:15 | Open **Before you start** | "First, gather the 3 required documents and your key numbers." |
| 0:30 | Press **Fill with sample applicant** and scroll; point to the progress bar | "Fill in the form. The bar at the top shows what's left. Your answers are saved on this device for 7 days." |
| 0:50 | Press **?** next to **Total budget** | "Stuck? Press the question mark. You get a plain explanation, an example, common mistakes and the Invest Detroit source." |
| 1:10 | Change housing units to 8 and the loan to 2,000,000, then press **Review My Application** | "When you're done, press Review My Application." |
| 1:25 | Point to each group | "Red must be fixed before you can submit. Orange: please double-check. Blue: tips." |
| 1:40 | Press **Go to field**, fix the loan to 600,000 and review again | "Go to field takes you straight to the problem." |
| 1:55 | Press **My answer is correct: explain why** and type "Two units are market-rate." | "If an answer is right, explain it in one sentence. Your note goes with your application." |
| 2:10 | Open the chat and ask "Will I be approved?" | "Limitation: the assistant never predicts approval or your rate. Only Invest Detroit's team decides." |
| 2:25 | Press **Submit**; show **What happens next** | "Submit, and see the next steps and typical timing. Questions? info@investdetroit.com." |
